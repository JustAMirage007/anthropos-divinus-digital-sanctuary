The Vision

One Platform. Many Disciplines.

Anthropos Divinus — The Digital Sanctuary is a developing knowledge and research platform conceptualized by John Henry Douglas III, Independent Research Architect. Its purpose is to organize research, preserve documented sources, and provide reproducible mathematical and interpretive tools.

Historical evidence, mathematical derivations, scientific claims and symbolic interpretations are presented as distinct categories.
